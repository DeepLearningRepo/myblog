## Welcome to Axis's Ideas

This is a small space for thoughts worth keeping: a discovery in a piece of code, a question that came up while reading, or a moment from everyday life.

Writing makes a vague idea a little clearer. A real question is often a better starting point than waiting for a perfect article.

## What you will find here

- **Technical explorations**: questions, experiments, and the ideas behind tools and systems.
- **Reading and reflection**: notes from books and a record of changing perspectives.
- **Everyday notes**: small, specific things that are easy to forget.

## About this space

An idea does not need to be complete to belong here. A question, an experiment, or a short note can be the beginning of something worth exploring.
